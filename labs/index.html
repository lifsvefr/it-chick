Labs

Hands-on sandboxes

Lessons explain; labs let you try things for real and tell you exactly what happened. Nothing you do here can break anything.

🧮Formula LabWrite real spreadsheet formulas on a small sheet. The lab checks them on hidden data too, and explains each mistake in plain words. 🌐Web LabWrite real HTML, CSS and JavaScript and watch the page change. The checker runs your code in a real browser page and points to the exact line when something’s off. 🐍Python LabWrite and run real Python right in your browser, with nothing to install. The checker tries your program with different inputs and explains error messages in plain words. 📚Citation LabBuild references and in-text citations in APA, MLA and Chicago, and fix the ones that are wrong. The checker goes part by part and says exactly what’s off. 🀄Kanji LabA few minutes of spaced review a day: hiragana and katakana, then JLPT N5–N3 kanji and words. Every reading and meaning is checked against the KANJIDIC2 and JMdict dictionaries. 📈Data LabAsk a database questions in SQL, and analyse data and draw charts in R, right in your browser. The checker runs your answer on a second dataset too.
